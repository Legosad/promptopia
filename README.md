## Promptopia

Promptopia is a clever word play on Zootopia, as a world of Prompts. In the leading world of AI what better resource than a global storage of shared important prompts depending on any career or interest. While LLMs are used quite often by everyone, The skill of proper prompt engineering is still yet to be equipped by the majority.

Promptopia is an application where people can share their important, most regularly used prompts for others to find. Every prompt has a tag as a subject for people to search and find efficiently.

Users can login using google account and perform all CRUD operations for their prompts. However, to view and copy any prompt there is no login or registration required as knowledge should be a free resource.

I stumbled upon this self learning project on one of my favourite channels on youtube [Javascript Mastery](https://www.youtube.com/@javascriptmastery) while I was looking into Next.js.

## Getting Started

In this project I am using a React framework called Next with javascript and tailwindcss, MongoDB and serverless routes because of Next. It is also my first time using Google O-Auth as NextAuth Providers.

The easiest way to deploy your Next.js app is to use the [Vercel Platform](https://vercel.com/new?utm_medium=default-template&filter=next.js&utm_source=create-next-app&utm_campaign=create-next-app-readme) from the creators of Next.js.

I deployed my application on vercel as well and you can find it here > [My Promptopia App](https://promptopia-red-theta.vercel.app/)
